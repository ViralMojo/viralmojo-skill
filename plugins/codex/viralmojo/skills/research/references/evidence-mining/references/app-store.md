# App Store reviews

The strongest complaint channel for mobile products: quantified ratings, switcher language, and the "good but…" gap between promise and delivery. Works for the brand's own app or a competitor's — anything with a public App Store listing. No listing, no channel.

## 1. Locate the listing

You need the app's store URL; the website crawl usually surfaces it. Without one, a keyword search for the product's store listing finds it.

## 2. Pick countries

Pull the public review feed per country. Default: **us, gb, au**. Expand when the website crawl surfaced localization signals — Chinese content → cn, tw; Japanese → jp; EUR pricing → de, fr. A brand localizing into a market has customers venting in that market's storefront.

## 3. Read in evidence order

Most-helpful reviews first — a review the community voted helpful is pre-validated, always top tier. Then recent, for freshness on current complaints.

## 4. Bucket what you read

- **Complaints** — 1–2★ plus the negative half of 3★. The raw objection material.
- **Loves** — 4–5★ patterns. What actually lands, in customer words.
- **Switching language** — any rating carrying markers like *switched from*, *moved from*, *used to use*, *replaced*, *came from*, *left X for*. Highest-signal bucket.
- **"Good but…"** — 3★ reviews that praise then pivot, plus the *"only thing I wish…"* lines buried in 4★ praise. The sharpest marketing signal in the channel: the customer wanted to love it and names exactly where it fell short.

## 5. Rank and attribute

- Tiers: most-helpful = top; substantive mid-rating text (3★+ with real length) = mid; else long-tail. Ranking only.
- Source strings name the storefront country, the star rating, and the helpful count.

## Thin harvest: one re-angle, then stop

If the reviews come back thin, the listing simply has little volume — re-pulling the same storefronts won't manufacture more. Add countries **once** when a real localization signal warrants it; otherwise report the thin harvest plainly. Repeated pulls re-read the same feeds and spend the user's money re-proving the same read.
